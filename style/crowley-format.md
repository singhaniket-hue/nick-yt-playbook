# Dark-documentary format parameters

These reusable targets summarize the format research in
`docs/dark-documentary-playbook.md`. They are editorial defaults, not claims
about a particular episode or voice.

| Parameter | Target | Observed range |
|---|---:|---:|
| Runtime | 34 min | 27:17–42:32 |
| Narration speed | 177 WPM | 166.3–191.0 |
| Word count | ~6,010 | 4,537–7,365 |
| Average shot length | 3.12s | 2.95–3.35s |
| Archival / real footage | 65% | — |
| Screenshots / artifacts | 20% | — |
| Motion graphics | 15% | — |
| Face cam | 0% | — |

## Voice calibration

Narration speed varies by voice, language mix, and spelling convention. Measure
the configured voice with `rabbithole timing` on at least 400 words before
changing the act budgets; short pilots exaggerate fixed leading and trailing
silence.

## Act budgets at 177 WPM against 34 minutes

| Act | Window | Words | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| I — Cold open | 0:00–0:30 | 90 | Immediate disruption; no greeting or channel intro |
| II — Origin / baseline | 0:30–3:00 | 440 | Mundane context, then the first anomaly |
| III — Rabbit hole | 3:00–22:00 | 3,360 | Chronological deep dive, chapters, and re-hooks every 3–5 min |
| IV — Climax | 22:00–31:00 | 1,590 | Darkest payload, preceded by a silence drop |
| V — Unresolved outro | 31:00–34:00 | 530 | No neat closure; reframe, then cut to black |

The validator allows ±15% per act.

## Pattern-interrupt cadence

| Interval | Dimension | Execution |
|---|---|---|
| 3–5s | Visual | Glitch cut or restrained scale punch-in on screenshots |
| 10–15s | Audio | Static burst, glitch sting, or metal scrape |
| 45–60s | Tension | Silence drop or heartbeat swell |
| 4.5–8 min | Structure | Case-card title and new chapter |
